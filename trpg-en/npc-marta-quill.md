# Marta Quill

Harbourmaster of Port Harrow. She ferries supplies to the rock but has not set foot there since 1919.

:::coc-stat{name="Marta Quill" role="Harbourmaster, 46" str=55 con=70 siz=60 dex=60 int=55 app=45 pow=50 edu=45 san=50 hp=13 db=0 build=0 move=8}
**Skills:** Persuade 55%, Navigate 60%, Pilot Boat 80%, Psychology 40%, Spot Hidden 50%.

**Attack:** Fighting (Brawl) 40%, boathook 1d6+DB.
:::

## Playing her

- Warm, practical and evasive. She asks the investigators more questions than they ask her.
- She names a crossing price of $12, which is double the going rate, and she knows it.
- If pressed on 1919 she goes quiet and hands over the [receipts](clue-oil-receipts.md).

## What she knows

She delivers food but never oil. She stopped landing on the rock after hearing the humming at night, and she has told no one.
