# Ambrose Vale

The keeper of the Gull Rock light. Thin, polite, and a little too rested for a man who has not left his post in three years.

:::coc-stat{name="Ambrose Vale" role="Lighthouse keeper, 58" str=50 con=60 siz=65 dex=45 int=70 app=50 pow=75 edu=60 san=55 hp=12 db=0 build=0 move=5}
**Skills:** Listen 60%, Spot Hidden 55%, Navigate 70%, Mechanical Repair 65%, Library Use 45%, Cthulhu Mythos 12%.

**Attack:** Fighting (Brawl) 35%, 1d3 damage. He would rather talk.
:::

## Playing him

- He answers every question honestly and never hurries. The horror is that he is not lying.
- He refers to the thing below as "the Tenant" and to himself as "the previous keeper's lodger".
- He will not leave the lamp room after dusk.

## What he knows

The light keeps the Tenant asleep. Every keeper before him knew it. The Board does not.
