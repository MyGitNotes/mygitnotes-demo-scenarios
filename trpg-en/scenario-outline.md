# The Keeper of Gull Rock

*Players: 3–4 investigators · Length: one evening (about 3 hours) · Era: Massachusetts coast, October 1925*

## Premise

The Coast Lights Board employs the investigators to find out why Ambrose Vale, keeper of the Gull Rock light, has not answered the monthly signal for nine days. The lamp still burns every night on time. Nobody in Port Harrow will row out to see why.

## Act I: Port Harrow

The investigators arrive by the afternoon train and learn that the village does not want to talk about the rock.

- [Marta Quill](npc-marta-quill.md), the harbourmaster, is friendly and keeps changing the subject. She will sell a boat crossing for a price that is too high.
- The Board's letter and a clipping from the *Harrow Courier* are in the [handouts](handouts.md).
- Good research rolls turn up the [oil receipts](clue-oil-receipts.md): Vale has bought no lamp oil all year.

## Act II: The crossing

A fog comes in half way across. The boat bumps against the jetty on [Gull Rock](map-gull-rock.md).

- The lighthouse door is open. The kitchen table is laid for two.
- The [logbook pages](clue-logbook-pages.md) show entries in two different hands.
- In the fog the investigators hear [a low humming](clue-humming.md) that seems to come from under their feet.

## Act III: The lamp room

[Ambrose Vale](npc-ambrose-vale.md) is alive and calm. He explains that the lamp does not burn oil, and that what sleeps under the rock only stays asleep while the light turns.

The investigators must choose:

1. Leave the light running and take Vale back to the mainland, which condemns the next keeper.
2. Put the light out, which wakes the thing below. Use [the lower door](clue-lower-door.md) to show them what that means.
3. Stay as keepers themselves.

## Sanity triggers

| Moment | Loss on failure | Loss on success |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hearing the humming for the first time | 1d4 | 0 |
| Opening the lower door | 1d6 | 1 |
| Seeing Vale's second logbook hand match their own | 1d3 | 0 |

## Rolls at a glance

| Clue | Skill | Difficulty | If they fail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Oil receipts](clue-oil-receipts.md) | Library Use, or Persuade (Marta) | Regular | Marta hands them over anyway after the boat price is settled |
| [Logbook pages](clue-logbook-pages.md) | Spot Hidden | Regular | Vale gives them the logbook himself in Act III |
| [The humming](clue-humming.md) | Listen | Hard while talking, otherwise Regular | They hear it only when the fog thickens |
| [The lower door](clue-lower-door.md) | Spot Hidden | Hard | Vale shows them |

A clue the table needs to continue is never behind a failed roll. Let a failure cost time, a point of Luck or a little Sanity, not the story. Allow one pushed roll per clue; a failed push brings the fog in early.

## Keeper's notes

Keep the pace of a ghost story, not a fight. If the table is quick, cut the crossing. If the table is slow, let the fog do the work.
