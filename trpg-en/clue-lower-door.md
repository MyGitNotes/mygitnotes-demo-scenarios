# Clue: the lower door

**Found in:** the lighthouse, behind the oil store. **Roll:** Spot Hidden (hard), or Vale shows them.

An iron door, warm to the touch, with a keyhole shaped like a lamp wick. There is no oil in the store because there never needs to be.

**What it means:** the final choice. Opening it shows the investigators what the light is for. Use it to make the ending a decision, not a fight.

**Sanity:** 1/1d6.

See the endings in the [outline](scenario-outline.md) and the [map](map-gull-rock.md).
