# Player handouts

Show these to the players when the story reaches them. The *Editor note* on each one is the Keeper's reminder and stays off the player copy.

:::handout{id="DOC-01" title="Letter from the Coast Lights Board" variant="letter" keeper="Give this at the start. The signature is real; the Board only knows Vale as reliable."}
Port Harrow, 14 October 1925

Gentlemen,

The keeper of Gull Rock, Mr Ambrose Vale, has not returned his monthly signal. The lamp is reported to burn as usual.

We ask that you sail to the rock, determine his condition, and report to this office. Expenses will be met on presentation of receipts.

Yours faithfully,
H. Ferris, Secretary
:::

:::handout{id="DOC-02" title="Harrow Courier, 4 June 1919" variant="newspaper" keeper="Hand over in Act I once someone asks about the harbourmaster's past."}
**Boat Lost Off Gull Rock**

The supply skiff *Dorothea* was found adrift on Tuesday morning with her two crew missing. The harbourmaster, Mrs Quill, who piloted the boat the previous day, has declined to comment.

The keeper at the time reported nothing unusual.
:::
