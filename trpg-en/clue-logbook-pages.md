# Clue: the logbook pages

**Found in:** the keeper's cottage, in the kitchen drawer. **Roll:** Spot Hidden.

Entries up to 8 October are in Vale's neat hand. After that a second hand takes over, writing the same short entries about fog and the light. The last page says *Do not let the lamp go out*.

**What it means:** someone, or something, has been keeping the log. The second hand matches the handwriting of the investigator who asked the most questions in Port Harrow (see [handout DOC-03](handouts.md)).

**Sanity:** 0/1d3 for that investigator.

**Points to:** [the humming](clue-humming.md).
