# Basis and dimension

What is a basis, and what is the dimension of a space?

---

A **basis** is a list of vectors that is [independent](card-independence.md) and [spans](card-span.md) the space.

The **dimension** is the number of vectors in any basis. For $\mathbb{R}^n$ it is $n$, because $(1,0,\dots,0), \dots, (0,\dots,0,1)$ is a basis.

---

## Why it matters

Every vector has exactly one set of coordinates in a given basis. Changing the basis changes the numbers, not the vector. That is what [linear maps](card-linear-map.md) act on.
