# Linear algebra, one card at a time

A first course in linear algebra, kept the way a student keeps it: [lecture notes](lecture-notes.md) with the maths written out, and one short **card** per idea. Every card links to the ideas it builds on, so the notes also form a map.

## What to try

1. **Study the cards.** Open the *Linear algebra flashcards* compilation under *Compilations* and press **Start studying**. Each card shows its question first; press *Show next page* to reach the answer. Rating a card moves it to the next stage in the review schedule.
2. **See the maths.** [Lecture notes](lecture-notes.md) uses KaTeX: inline formulas such as $\mathbf{v}\in\mathbb{R}^n$ and display equations.
3. **Open the graph.** Open the *Concept map* compilation, or the Graph page: every concept card is a node, and the links between ideas are the edges. Start from [Vectors](card-vector.md) and follow the arrows to [Eigenvalues](card-eigenvalue.md).
4. **Add a card.** Start from any card: the first page is the question, and every page after a `---` line is an answer page.
5. **Find the eigenvalues on a curve.** At the end of the [lecture notes](lecture-notes.md), the polynomial of the worked example is drawn as a `line` chart; the eigenvalues are where it crosses zero.
6. **Edit freely.** This is a demo copy: your changes stay in your browser for 24 hours and never reach anyone else.
