# Returns

## Why

Sometimes a neighbour decides not to keep a parcel, or the shop has to send it back after it has waited too long. Today that is a scribble in the margin of the paper list.

## Rough idea

A *returned* status next to *waiting* and *collected*. The shopkeeper picks a reason, the neighbour is told, and the parcel leaves the shelf.

## Questions

- Does a return need a pickup code, like [collecting a parcel](spec-pickup-flow.md)?
- Who tells the carrier? Probably the shopkeeper, outside the app.
- How long should a parcel wait before it is eligible for return?
