# A designer's notebook: Mossgate Tea

This notebook is one freelance designer's working file for a small brand identity: a loose-leaf tea shop that needs a mark, a tin, a sign, an ordering app and a poster. Noor Hadley is the designer; the client, the shop and every person in it are invented.

## What to try

1. **Read the brief first.** [Brief](brief.md) says what was asked, what it costs and what is out of scope. Every later note answers it.
2. **Check the research.** [Research](research.md) is the two days of looking before any drawing.
3. **Click the pins and read the bars.** [Applications](applications.md) pins a note on the tin, the sign, the app screen and the poster, and [Round 2](round-2-refinement.md) shows the contrast of each colour pair as a bar chart against the 4.5 : 1 line.
4. **Edit freely.** This is a demo copy: your changes stay in your browser for 24 hours and never reach anyone else.
