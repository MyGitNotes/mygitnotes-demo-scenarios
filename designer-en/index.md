# A designer's notebook: Mossgate Tea

This notebook is one freelance designer's working file for a small brand identity: a loose-leaf tea shop that needs a mark, a tin, a sign, an ordering app and a poster. Noor Hadley is the designer; the client, the shop and every person in it are invented. The project follows the usual path of a branding job: brief, research, moodboard, rounds of explorations, critique, client feedback, final specs.

## What to try

1. **Look at the moodboard as a wall.** Open the **Moodboard wall** compilation under *Compilations*. It shows the four reference images as thumbnails next to the [moodboard note](moodboard.md), the way you would pin them up for a call.
2. **Compare three directions side by side.** Open **Round 1: three marks**. The three marks sit in medium cards, followed by the internal critique and the client's feedback.
3. **Read the feedback on the image.** [Client feedback on round 1](client-feedback-r1.md) pins each of Dana's six comments to the part of the mark it is about, and answers each number in a table, so a later round can point back to "comment 2". Comments that round 2 answered are marked resolved.
4. **See where the mark has to work.** **Applications** lays out the tin, the sign, the app screen and the poster as small cards. Click one to edit its note in place.
5. **Read the whole project as one book.** **Mossgate, start to finish** binds all eight notes into a single scroll, with the images where the notes put them.
6. **Change a status.** Each note carries a design status: *reference*, *exploring*, *in review* or *approved*. [Applications](applications.md) and the [handoff checklist](handoff.md) are still *in review*.
7. **Click the pins and read the bars.** [Applications](applications.md) pins a note on the tin, the sign, the app screen and the poster, and [Round 2](round-2-refinement.md) shows the contrast of each colour pair as a bar chart against the 4.5 : 1 line.
8. **Edit freely.** This is a demo copy: your changes stay in your browser for 24 hours and never reach anyone else.
