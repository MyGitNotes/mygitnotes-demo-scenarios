# Lesson plan: photosynthesis

**Class:** Grade 7 · **Length:** 45 minutes · **Room:** Science lab 2

## Goal

By the end, every student can say what a plant needs, what it makes, and why it matters for animals too.

## Common mistakes to listen for

- "Plants eat soil." The soil gives water and minerals, not the food. Most of a plant's dry mass comes from carbon dioxide in the air.
- "Plants only make oxygen." They also respire, using oxygen and releasing carbon dioxide, all day and night.
