# How plants make food

:::summary{slide="1"}
A plant makes its own food from light, water and air.
:::

---

## What goes in, what comes out

::::grid{cols="2"}
:::cell
### In
- Sunlight
- Water, from the roots
- Carbon dioxide, from the air
:::
:::cell
### Out
- Sugar (the plant's food)
- Oxygen (released to the air)
:::
::::

---

## The process

::::flow{direction="horizontal" arrow="→"}
:::step{icon="☀"}
**Light** is caught by chlorophyll in the leaf
:::
:::step{icon="💧"}
**Water** rises from the roots
:::
:::step{icon="🌿"}
**Chemistry** in the chloroplast joins them with CO₂
:::
:::step{icon="🍬"}
**Sugar** is made, **oxygen** is let go
:::
::::

---

## Compare: day and night

::::grid{cols="3"}
:::cell
**Daytime**

Photosynthesis runs. Oxygen leaves, carbon dioxide enters.
:::
:::cell
**Night**

No light, so no photosynthesis. The plant still uses some sugar for energy.
:::
:::cell
**Takeaway**

Plants do both: they make food in light and use food all the time.
:::
::::

---

:::
