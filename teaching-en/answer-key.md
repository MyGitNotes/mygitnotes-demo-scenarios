# Answer key

1. Light, water and carbon dioxide.
2. In photosynthesis it takes in carbon dioxide and gives out oxygen.
3. In the chloroplasts, which contain chlorophyll.
4. It turns pale or yellow, because without light it cannot make chlorophyll, and it grows tall and spindly as it reaches for light. It also weakens, because it cannot make sugar and has to use up what it stored.
5. Plants make the sugar that starts every food chain, and the oxygen that animals breathe.
