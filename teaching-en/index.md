# Teaching one lesson: how plants make food

A grade 7 science lesson on photosynthesis, kept as notes a teacher can both plan from and present from. The school and the class are made up.

## What to try

1. **Open the slides.** [Lesson slides](lesson-slides.md) lays the lesson out with slide directives: a `summary` box for the one-line takeaway, a `grid` of `cell` blocks for side-by-side comparison, and a `flow` of `step` blocks for the process. Horizontal rules (`::vertical`) mark where one slide ends.
2. **Change a slide.** Open the note in source mode and edit a `step`, or change `cols="2"` to `cols="3"` in the grid and add a cell.
3. **Plan from the notes.** [Lesson plan](lesson-plan.md) has timings; [Worksheet](worksheet.md) has the practice questions with a checklist; [Answer key](answer-key.md) has the answers.
4. **See where the 45 minutes go.** [Lesson plan](lesson-plan.md) shows the lesson time as a `parts` bar under the timing table.
5. **Edit freely.** This is a demo copy: your changes stay in your browser for 24 hours and never reach anyone else.
