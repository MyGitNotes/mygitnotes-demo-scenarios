# A week of daily notes

This notebook is one person's week, kept as a daily journal: one note per day, tasks with dates, and a weekly review that gathers the days together. Mira is a freelance illustrator finishing a client project; every detail here is invented.

## What to try

1. **Read the week as one page.** Open the **Week 43 in one scroll** compilation under *Compilations*. It collects every note tagged `week-43` into a book you can read top to bottom.
2. **Check a task off.** Open [Friday](journal/2026-10-23.md) and tick one of the boxes. The note stores the date it was finished next to the task.
3. **See every task at once.** Open the to-do tool in the right rail. Overdue, today and upcoming tasks from all notes sit in one list, and tasks with a start date also show as bars in its Gantt view.
4. **Write the review.** [Weekly review](weekly-review.md) is where the week gets summed up. Change its status from *working* to *done* when you finish it.
5. **Read the week in numbers.** [Weekly review](weekly-review.md) opens with three key numbers and a bar chart of the tasks ticked off each day.
6. **Edit freely.** This is a demo copy: your changes stay in your browser for 24 hours and never reach anyone else.
