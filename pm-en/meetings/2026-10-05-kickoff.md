# Kickoff

## Goal

A neighbour hears that a parcel has arrived and gets a code. The shopkeeper hands the parcel over by checking the code. Release day is Thursday 12 November, which is five weeks and three days away.

## What we agreed

- The scope is the pickup message, a reminder and the code check. Barcode scanning and returns stay on the backlog.
- There will be five milestones, written up as notes that show on the calendar. The dates are fixed backwards from release day.
- Each area has one accountable name. [Team and who owns what](../team.md) has the list.
- @maren writes a weekly status on Fridays, and there is a fifteen-minute sync on Mondays.
