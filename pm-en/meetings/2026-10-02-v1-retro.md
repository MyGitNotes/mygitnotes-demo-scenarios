# v1.0 retro

**Friday 2 October 2026, 16:00, forty-five minutes.** All six. Version 1.0 shipped on Thursday 24 September; this is what we change for 1.1. The notes were written up on Monday 5 October.

## What went well

- The check-in screen was tested with three shops before release, and none of them needed a hint.
- The release itself took twenty minutes, because @ines had written the steps down once, in a hurry.
- Support heard from four shops in the first week, and three were questions about a feature we are planning.

## What did not

- Store review took three days, not the one day we planned for, and the release slipped a day.
- Nobody owned the store submission. It was done by whoever had time, and the screenshots were the old ones.
- The shops that phoned us were the ones who had read nothing.

## Start, stop, continue

| Start | Stop | Continue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A three-working-day buffer for store review | Releasing without a named owner for the stores | Testing with real shops before release |
| Rolling out in steps, not all at once | Sending the release note to everyone on the day | Writing the release steps down |
| A phone call the day before to the pilot shops | | A short weekly status |
