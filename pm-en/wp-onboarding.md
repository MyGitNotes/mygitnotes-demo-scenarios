# Shop onboarding

**Owner:** @leo. **Accountable:** @maren.

Cubbyshelf lives or dies at the counter of a small shop, and a shopkeeper does not read release notes. The change on Thursday is one new screen and one new habit: ask for the six digits. The plan is three pilot shops that see it a week early, a one-page help sheet taped next to the till, and a phone call the day before.

## Done when

- Three pilot shops are chosen, with Hale's Corner Shop first, and two of them have run the counter flow once with @kofi in the shop.
- A one-page help sheet exists in print and as a screen inside the app.
- Every shop that has the app hears about the change before Thursday, by message or by phone.

## Notes

The v1.0 retro found that the shops that called us were the ones who had read nothing. This time the message that goes out the Monday before says three things only: what is new, what the neighbour will receive, and what the shopkeeper asks for.
