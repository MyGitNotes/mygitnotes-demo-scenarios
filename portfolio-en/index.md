# A portfolio, kept as notes

This notebook is the working copy of one designer's portfolio. Tamsin Verrill is a made-up freelancer who makes posters, small apps and picture series. Each page is written to be published as it stands. Every person, client and number here is invented.

## What to try

1. **Read the About page.** [About Tamsin](about.md) is the first page a visitor sees.
2. **Check the checklist.** [Case-study checklist](case-study-checklist.md) is the draft I hold every case study against before it goes up: problem, role, process, outcome.
3. **Click the pins and read the results.** [Twiglog](case-twiglog.md) pins notes on the wireframe sheet and the app screens, [Lamplight Hall](case-lamplight-hall.md) on the thumbnail sheet and the three posters, and both case studies end with their results as `key-numbers`, each with its before figure.
4. **Edit freely.** This is a demo copy: your changes stay in your browser for 24 hours and never reach anyone else.
