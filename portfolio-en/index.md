# A portfolio, kept as notes

This notebook is the working copy of one designer's portfolio. Tamsin Verrill is a made-up freelancer who makes posters, small apps and picture series. Each page is written to be published as it stands, and the work it shows is gathered into compilations, so a visitor can browse the pictures or read the whole site as one scroll. Every person, client and number here is invented.

## What to try

1. **Browse the selected work.** Open **Selected work** under *Compilations*. One picture and one page for each of the three projects sit in medium cards, a front door to the portfolio.
2. **Look at one project as a set.** **Lamplight Hall posters** shows the thumbnail sheet and the three finished posters. **Twiglog screens** shows the paper wireframes next to the final app screens. **Small Harbours** lays the day out from dawn to night.
3. **Read a case study.** [Lamplight Hall Film Nights](case-lamplight-hall.md) follows the shape most reviewers look for: problem, role, process, outcome. Its headings state what was found, and the pictures sit beside the sentences that need them.
4. **Read the whole site as one scroll.** **Portfolio site** binds About, both case studies, the series and the process page into a single book.
5. **Check what is still in progress.** Statuses run *draft*, *polishing*, *ready*. [Twiglog](case-twiglog.md) and [How I work](how-i-work.md) are still being polished, and the [case-study checklist](case-study-checklist.md) is the draft behind them.
6. **Click the pins and read the results.** [Twiglog](case-twiglog.md) pins notes on the wireframe sheet and the app screens, [Lamplight Hall](case-lamplight-hall.md) on the thumbnail sheet and the three posters, and both case studies end with their results as `key-numbers`, each with its before figure.
7. **Edit freely.** This is a demo copy: your changes stay in your browser for 24 hours and never reach anyone else.
