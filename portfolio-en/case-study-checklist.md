# Case-study checklist

A checklist for myself, not for the site. I run a draft past it before a case study goes from *polishing* to *ready*. It comes from reading what hiring managers and design writers say about portfolios (the Nielsen Norman Group's guidance on design portfolios, for one) and from the three I have already written.

## Before writing

- [ ] **One sentence for the whole project**, with a result in it. "Three posters that filled a village hall." If I cannot write it, the project is not ready to be a case study.
- [ ] **Three to five case studies in total.** Fewer, deeper ones. A fourth is only worth adding if it shows something the others do not.
- [ ] **Check what I may show.** Client's permission, NDAs, anything private in the images.

## The four parts every case study needs

1. **The problem.** What was the situation and why did it matter? Names of people and places, not "the client".
2. **My role.** What I did and, just as important, what I did not do. A reviewer who has to guess loses trust.
3. **The process.** The decisions, not the activities. Not "I did research", but what the research found and what I changed because of it.
4. **The outcome.** A number if there is a number; if there is not, what changed and what the client said. Say where each number came from.

## The details that make it good

- [ ] **Headings that state a finding.** "We drew nine ideas before we picked one" teaches more than "Ideation".
- [ ] **What I rejected, and why.** Hiring managers say outright that they want to know what is not in the design.
- [ ] **A picture next to every claim.** An image does the work of a paragraph when it sits beside the sentence that needs it.
- [ ] **Captions that say what to look at**, not what the image is.
- [ ] **Readable in about ninety seconds.** A skim should still find the problem, the role and the result.

## After writing

- [ ] Read the first screen as a stranger. Is it clear what the project was, what I did and why anyone should care?
- [ ] Read it aloud once. Cut every sentence I would not say.
- [ ] Change the status to *ready* and add it to the site book.
