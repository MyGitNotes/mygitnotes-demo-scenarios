# Tamsin Verrill

Designer and illustrator. I make posters, small apps and picture series for people who run things on a shoestring: volunteer cinemas, field-study groups, harbour villages.

## What I do

- **Print and identity.** Posters, labels and small identity systems, usually in two or three inks.
- **Product design.** Interfaces for apps used outside, in bad light, by people with something else on their mind.
- **Illustration.** Gouache and screen-print pictures, commissioned or made for my own series.

## Recent work

1. [Lamplight Hall Film Nights](case-lamplight-hall.md): a poster series that filled a village hall.
2. [Twiglog](case-twiglog.md): a field journal for bird watchers, with buttons big enough for touchscreen gloves.
3. [Small Harbours](small-harbours.md): one harbour painted at four times of day.

## Working with me

I take two or three projects at a time and send a fixed quote after a first call. The way a project runs is on [How I work](how-i-work.md).

I am taking new projects from January.
