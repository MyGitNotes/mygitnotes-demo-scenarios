# Freight Services Agreement

**Between** Larkmoor Freight Co., a Maine corporation ("Provider"), **and** Tallowfield Bakery Group, Inc., a Maine corporation ("Client").
**Effective date:** November 1, 2026.

## 1. Services

1.1 The Provider collects the Client's baked goods from the Tallowfield plant and delivers them to up to 12 retail locations in the Harbor District, on weekdays.

1.2 The Provider supplies refrigerated vehicles and drivers, and tells the Client of any delay within one hour.

## 2. Fees and payment

2.1 The Client pays a fixed fee of $18,000 per month.

2.2 Invoices are payable within 60 days of the invoice date.

2.3 The Provider may suspend the service after 90 days of non-payment.

## 3. Term and termination

3.1 The initial term is 24 months from the effective date.

3.2 The agreement renews automatically for further 24-month terms unless either party gives notice at least 30 days before the term ends.

3.3 The Provider may terminate for convenience on 14 days' written notice.

## 4. Liability

4.1 The Provider's total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees paid in the month in which the claim arose.

4.2 The Provider is not liable for spoiled or damaged goods.

## 5. Confidentiality

Each party keeps the other's pricing, routes and customer lists confidential during the term and for two years after it ends.

## 6. Governing law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Maine. Disputes go to the courts of Harbor County.
