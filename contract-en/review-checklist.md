# Review checklist for freight contracts

The questions asked of every draft, whoever wrote it. Check them off while reading the [agreement](services-agreement.md).

- [x] Who are the parties, and are the legal names right?
- [x] What exactly is delivered, where, and how often?
- [x] When is payment due, and what happens when it is late?
- [x] How does the term end, and does it renew by itself?
- [x] Can both sides leave, or only one?
- [x] What is the liability cap, and what does it cover?
- [x] Who carries the risk of spoiled goods?
- [ ] Is the insurance certificate attached to the file?
- [ ] Has the partner signed off on the open points in [Clause changes](clause-changes.md)?

Add your own checks; this list is plain Markdown.
