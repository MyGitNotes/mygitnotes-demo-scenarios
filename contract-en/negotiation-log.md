# Negotiation log

The conversations behind each version of the [agreement](services-agreement.md).

## October 5: draft received

Larkmoor's sales lead sent the draft by email with a request to sign within two weeks. It became **v1**.

## October 6: internal review

Priya went through the draft with the operations manager. Five points came out of it, and the review comments went into the note.

## October 8: redline sent

The five points went back to Larkmoor as **v2**. No reply for four days.

## October 12: call with Larkmoor

Larkmoor accepted a mutual termination right and the refrigeration clause. It refused the 12-month cap as out of proportion to an $18,000-a-month contract. We agreed six months, a late charge, shorter notice periods and an insurance clause. The result is **v3**.

### Why the cap ended at six months

The clause that took the most arguing, drawn as a map. Priya's reasons come from her review comments on v1 and v3, Larkmoor's from the call.

::::argument-map{title="Where the liability cap landed" source="Review comments on v1 and v3; call with Larkmoor, October 12"}
- [C] **Cap Larkmoor's liability at the greater of $108,000 and six months of fees**｜v3, clause 4.1

:::reason{to="C" id="R1" label="Why not one month"}
- [T1] One month of fees is $18,000, less than one day's goods for all twelve shops in peak season｜Priya, comment on v1
:::

:::reason{to="C" id="R2" label="Why not twelve"}
- [B1] Twelve months of fees, $216,000, is out of proportion to an $18,000-a-month contract｜Larkmoor, October 12
:::

:::reason{to="C" id="R3" label="Why six months, with a floor"}
- [F1] Six months of fees is $108,000, and the cap never falls below that figure, even early in the term｜Priya, comment on v3
- [F2] $1,000,000 of cargo insurance (clause 4.3) means the capped amount will actually be paid｜Priya, comment on 4.3
:::

:::objection{to="C" id="O1" label="Half of what we asked for" status="open"}
- Six months is half of the twelve we asked for in v2, and the partner has not signed off｜[Clause changes](clause-changes.md), "Still open"
:::
::::
