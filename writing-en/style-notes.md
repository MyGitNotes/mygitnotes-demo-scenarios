# Style notes

Rules the author set for the whole book, so the chapters sound like one person.

- **Open on a scene, not a thesis.** Every chapter begins with something someone did or said.
- **Numbers get a person.** A figure from the ledger is always attached to a name or a day.
- **Fair to both sides.** The bridge's supporters and the ferry's defenders each get their best argument in their own words.
- **No verdict.** The last chapter lays out the choice. The reader decides.
- **Short paragraphs.** Aim for under 120 words, and one idea each.

See the [outline](book.outline.md) for the order of chapters.
