# Chapter 4: The vote

The council met on a Wednesday, in a room that smelled of floor polish. Forty-odd residents came, which was more than for the last three budgets together.

Marguerite Oyelaran spoke first for the bridge. She runs the island's only clinic and has twice watched a helicopter wait on weather. "A road does not need a captain's permission," she said.

Haakon Deverell answered for the ferry. He pointed out that the bridge would bring the island's first traffic jam, and its second supermarket.

Neither of them was wrong about anything they said.
